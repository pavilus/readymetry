# Question Sources

This register records the materials used to plan and verify Readymetry questions.
Source access does not automatically make a question publishable. Every question
still requires an original stem, original distractors, an explanation, and review.

| Source | Access | Appropriate use | Restrictions |

## Welding Handbook Volume 4 coverage map

The official preview identifies these major chapters:

1. Carbon and Low-Alloy Steels
2. High-Alloy Steels
3. Coated Steels
4. Tool and Die Steels
5. Stainless and Heat-Resistant Steels
6. Clad and Dissimilar Metals
7. Surfacing Materials
8. Cast Irons
9. Maintenance and Repair Welding
10. Underwater Welding and Cutting

For CWI Part A growth, prioritize original questions in:

- Carbon and low-alloy steel fundamentals
- Weld-related cracking
- Quenched and tempered steels
- Heat-treatable low-alloy steels
- Stainless steel families and weldability
- Dissimilar-metal welding variables
- Cast iron metallurgy and weldability
- Repair planning, failure cause documentation, and procedure establishment

Obtain the relevant complete chapter or another authoritative complete reference
before publishing detailed questions in these areas.
